## Poly Haven assets

SPDX license identifier: `CC0-1.0`

License: CC0 1.0 Universal

Source: [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/)

The Anatta Worlds beauty slice vendors optimized derivatives of these assets:

- `aerial_grass_rock`: ground diffuse, normal and ARM maps.
- `rocky_terrain_03`: rock diffuse and normal maps.
- `weathered_planks`: wood diffuse and normal maps.
- `fir_sapling`: game-ready Meshopt GLB with three instanced tree variants.
- `mountainside`: game-ready Meshopt GLB used for instanced cliff outcrops.
- `rock_moss_set_02`: game-ready Meshopt/WebP GLB used for instanced riparian rock clusters.
- `boulder_01`: decimated game-ready Meshopt/WebP GLB used for larger shoreline boulders.
- `overcast_soil_puresky`: environment HDRI.
