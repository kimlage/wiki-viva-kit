# Overlay - root company

Use this overlay when the root entity is a company, institution or large
organization.

## Quadrant Placement Guardrail

- Units, departments, governance forums and reporting lines are not Q3 by
  default. They are Q3 only when preserving culture, shared meaning,
  stakeholder trust, role expectations or relationship context; as formal
  operating structure they are Q4.
- Systems of record, CRM/ERP, document systems, support systems, calendars and
  process cadences are Q4. Reports, products, assets and other owned outputs are
  Q2 only when treated as observable evidence/output of the company.
