# Overlay - root team

Use this overlay when the root entity is a team.

## Quadrant Placement Guardrail

- Team membership, org charts, RACI rows and workflow assignments are not Q3 by
  themselves. They are Q3 only when the page captures shared meaning,
  roles-as-lived, mutual expectations, norms or relationship context; as
  administered structure they are Q4.
- Repositories, tools and boards are Q2 only when the fact is the team's owned
  output/evidence. The platform, workflow, CI, Jira/board, chat channel or
  release governance that coordinates work is Q4.
