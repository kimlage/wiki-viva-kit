## Question

Can one person complete the entire operational loop in three to five minutes,
with the same selection surviving 3D to 2D to 3D and a receipt producing one
localized, truthful world mutation?

Use one intention, one synthetic source with two observed states, one
perspective, one tension, one real-world mission, returned evidence, one human
gate and one receipt. Provenance is structural; decorative terrain is optional.

The slice succeeds only if removing 3D harms orientation rather than beauty,
removing 2D harms precision rather than atmosphere, and the user can distinguish
observed state, proposal, approval and consequence without a tutorial.
