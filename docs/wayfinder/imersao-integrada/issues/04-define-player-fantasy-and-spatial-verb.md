Type: grilling
Mode: HITL

## Question

What should the player repeatedly do in the spatial world that would lose
meaning if the 3D were removed?

Recommended starting answer: the player **tends a living situation** by noticing
a localized tension, bringing it into focus, acting outside the interface and
returning with evidence that changes that same place.

This ticket must resolve one canonical verb phrase and reject nearby fantasies
such as collecting, conquering, decorating or merely browsing a dashboard.
