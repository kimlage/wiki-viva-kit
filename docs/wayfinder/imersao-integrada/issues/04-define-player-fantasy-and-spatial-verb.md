# Define the player fantasy and spatial verb

Type: grilling
Mode: HITL
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 03
Owner: Kim's canonical GDD direction, compiled by Codex, 2026-07-30

## Question

What should the player repeatedly do in the spatial world that would lose
meaning if the 3D were removed?

## Answer

The player is the **guardian of a small living world**. The canonical verb
phrase for the prototype is:

> **Trace and tend one real tension from observed diff to receipt, then witness
> only the localized consequence that the receipt authorizes.**

The loop is spatial only where place carries orientation and consequence.
Evidence, alternatives, authority and the gate remain precise 2D operations.
Collecting, conquering, decorating, grinding and merely browsing a dashboard
are explicitly rejected by the GDD.

Kim's instruction that the latest Anatta Worlds direction governs this work
resolves the product authority. The three prototypes remain the HITL mechanism
for choosing the spatial embodiment of this verb.
